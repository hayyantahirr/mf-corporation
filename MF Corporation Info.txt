What Do We Do?
MF Express enables Pakistani shoppers to purchase goods from Chinese marketplace such as Alibaba, DHgate, Made in China, or other Chinese factories and deliver it to the MF Express warehouse without worrying about any hassle of the international shipping from China to Pakistan or custom clearances. MF Express aims to protect customers from expensive international delivery charges and deliver goods from China to their doorstep.

What is MF Express?
Our service offers a warehouse in China to Pakistani shoppers so that they can buy products from Chinese stores and factories and keep it stored at the MF Express
warehouse facilities. It saves the customers from paying the high warehousing fees to shipping companies and provides a safe place where they can store their goods until the shipment is consolidated. MF Express then consolidates the shipment and get it shipped to Pakistan. Once the shipment arrives in Pakistan, MF Express takes care of all the logistics and custom clearances until the parcel gets delivered to the customer’s doorstep. MF Express is an easy, affordable, and one-stop solution for all to ship from China.




Info About MF Packages 
MF Packages is a flexible packaging provider that offers custom branding, printing, and global fulfilment solutions for growing businesses. With a company history dating back to 2018 and a team of over 50 skilled workers, the company operates out of Karachi, Pakistan, while supporting shipping and delivery for both local and international markets. Their core role is to help emerging brands, retailers, food businesses, distributors, and bulk buyers select packaging formats that balance product protection, usability, visual identity, and practical order requirements.

The company offers a wide variety of both ready-made and custom-manufactured packaging formats. Their pouch range includes stand-up pouches for snacks and dry foods, natural-looking kraft paper pouches with optional clear windows and resealable zippers, high-barrier aluminium pouches to protect against light, oxygen, and moisture, and transparent plastic pouches for product visibility. They also supply flat-bottom pouches for stable shelf presentation, heat-processable retort pouches for prepared foods, spout pouches for liquids and semi-liquids, specialized coffee pouches with valves and zippers, coffee brewing filters, and shrink capsules for bottle finishing and closure.

Beyond stock items, MF Packages provides customization options covering brand identity, dimensions, closures, finishes, and barrier performance based on a product's storage conditions and target shelf life. Their manufacturing and printing model combines HP Indigo digital presses (such as the 25K, 20000s, and 6900s series) for short-run or variable-design projects alongside Heidelberg and Roland offset systems, CTP pre-press, binding, gluing, UV coating, and lamination. While they highlight sustainable and kraft-paper options, the profile advises clients to verify food-contact compliance, material structures, and environmental claims against full technical data sheets and current certificates rather than assuming a paper outer layer makes a multilayer pouch recyclable or biodegradable.

MF Packages serves several sectors, including food and snacks, coffee and beverages, prepared foods, and personal care or specialty retail. They work with four main types of clients: bulk brand buyers, small businesses or startups looking for stock or entry-level custom options, online or retail resellers, and regional or wholesale distributors. To take an order from initial inquiry to delivery, they follow a five-step process that involves understanding the product brief, recommending a suitable format, confirming artwork and samples, approving commercial terms like pricing and minimum order quantities, and finally producing and dispatching the goods.
