Info ABout mf express 
What Do We Do?
MF Express enables Pakistani shoppers to purchase goods from Chinese marketplace such as Alibaba, DHgate, Made in China, or other Chinese factories and deliver it to the MF Express warehouse without worrying about any hassle of the international shipping from China to Pakistan or custom clearances. MF Express aims to protect customers from expensive international delivery charges and deliver goods from China to their doorstep.

What is MF Express?
Our service offers a warehouse in China to Pakistani shoppers so that they can buy products from Chinese stores and factories and keep it stored at the MF Express
warehouse facilities. It saves the customers from paying the high warehousing fees to shipping companies and provides a safe place where they can store their goods until the shipment is consolidated. MF Express then consolidates the shipment and get it shipped to Pakistan. Once the shipment arrives in Pakistan, MF Express takes care of all the logistics and custom clearances until the parcel gets delivered to the customer’s doorstep. MF Express is an easy, affordable, and one-stop solution for all to ship from China.
